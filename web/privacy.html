Kindred Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Kindred collects only the information needed to run your account, sync your reading progress and power reading groups. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

This policy applies to the Kindred mobile app (the "App"), developed by Abdul Gany ("we", "us"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

DataWhat it includesWhy
Account informationEmail address, display name, and a unique user IDTo create and secure your account
Reading activityBooks in your library, reading position (locations within a book), progress percentages and reading statisticsTo sync your place across devices and show your stats
Group activityGroups you create or join, your active group, books assigned to a group, and your progress within themTo run shared reading groups and show members each other's progress
Device and diagnostic dataDevice type, OS version, app version, crash logs and app usage statisticsTo fix bugs, keep the App stable and understand how it is used

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

Book files. Kindred does not provide books. You add your own EPUB files, which are opened and rendered on your device.

How we use and share information

We use your information only to provide, maintain and improve the App. We never sell or rent it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Other users. When you join a group, other members can see your display name, the group's books and your reading progress in them. Your personal library and statistics outside the group stay private.

Service providers. We use Google Firebase to store data and run the App:

  • Firebase Authentication — sign-in and account security
  • Cloud Firestore — storing your account, library, progress and group data
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics — crash reports and usage statistics

Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy terms. Data may be stored on servers outside your country.

Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time by emailing indeavourfeedback@gmail.com. Full instructions are on our account deletion page.

We delete your account, library, progress and statistics within 30 days of your request. Your progress in groups is removed; content other members created stays with them.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by Firebase. Access is restricted by security rules so users can read only their own data and data from groups they belong to. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Kindred is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under South Africa's POPIA and the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority, such as South Africa's Information Regulator.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the App.
