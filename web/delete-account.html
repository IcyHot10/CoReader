Delete your Kindred account

Kindred is developed by Abdul Gany. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time, even if you no longer have the app installed.

To request deletion, email:
indeavourfeedback@gmail.com
Email a deletion request

How it works

  1. Send the email from the address you use to sign in to Kindred, with the subject "Kindred account deletion request".
  2. We may reply to confirm the request comes from the account owner.
  3. We delete your account and data within 30 days and email you once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, display name and user ID
  • Your library, reading positions, progress and reading statistics
  • Your membership and progress in all reading groups

What we keep

  • Groups and group books created by other members stay with them; your name and progress are removed from them.
  • Anonymous crash and usage statistics, which are not linked to your account.

We don't keep any other copy of your data after deletion. The EPUB files you added stay on your device until you uninstall the app or delete them.

For more detail, see our Privacy Policy.
